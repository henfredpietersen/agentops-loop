# Invoices & statements

Invoices are emailed on the 1st of every month to the email address on your student profile.

## Statements
Download a full statement of account from Billing > Statements in the portal. Statements show every payment, fee and credit note.

## Tax invoices
If your employer needs a tax invoice with their VAT number, email billing with the company name and VAT number and we reissue the invoice within 2 business days.

## Proof of payment
Upload proof of payment for EFT transfers under Billing > Upload POP. Use your student number as the payment reference.
