# How to enrol

1. Apply online and choose your programme.
2. Upload a certified copy of your ID and your matric certificate.
3. Pay the deposit or upload your sponsor letter.

## Confirmation
Your enrolment is confirmed by email within 3 business days after your documents are verified. You then get your student number and LMS login.
