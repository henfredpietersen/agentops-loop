# Logging in to the LMS

## Forgot password
Use the Forgot password link on the login page. A reset link is sent to your student email and expires after 1 hour.

## Locked account
After 5 failed login attempts your account locks for 30 minutes. Wait 30 minutes and try again, or reset your password.

## Two-step verification
Two-step verification uses the authenticator app. If you change phones, contact support to reset two-step verification.
