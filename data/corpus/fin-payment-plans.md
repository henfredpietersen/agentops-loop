# Payment plans

Pay upfront or spread your fees.

## Monthly debit order
Pay a 20% deposit and the balance over 10 monthly debit orders. Debit orders run on the 1st or the 25th of the month, you choose on the application form.

## Missed payments
A missed debit order adds a R250 late payment fee. After two missed payments your LMS access is suspended until the arrears are paid.

## Changing your debit date or bank account
Update banking details in the portal under Billing > Payment method at least 5 business days before the next debit.
