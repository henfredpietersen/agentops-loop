# Employer and bursary sponsored students

If an employer or bursary pays your fees, upload a signed sponsor letter on company letterhead before enrolment closes.

### Third-party payer
The sponsor is invoiced directly and becomes the account payer. If the sponsor does not pay within 60 days, the student becomes liable for the balance.
