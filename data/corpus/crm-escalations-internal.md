# Escalation matrix (internal)

| Tier | Owner | Response time |
|------|-------|---------------|
| Tier 1 | Student support agent | 1 business day |
| Tier 2 | Team lead | 2 business days |
| Tier 3 | Head of student services | 5 business days |

Formal complaints not resolved at tier 3 can be referred to the student ombud.
