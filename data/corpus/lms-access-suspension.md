# Why is my course access suspended?

Course access is suspended when an account is in arrears or when a sponsor letter is missing.

## Getting access back
Access is restored automatically within 24 hours after the payment clears. EFT payments can take 2 business days to clear, so upload proof of payment to speed things up.
