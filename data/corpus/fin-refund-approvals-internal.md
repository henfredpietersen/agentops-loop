# STAFF ONLY - refund approvals workflow

## Approval limits
Agents may approve refunds up to R5 000. Refunds above R5 000 need finance manager approval in the approvals queue before payment.

## Chargebacks
If a student opens a card chargeback, freeze the refund request and tag the case CHARGEBACK-HOLD so it is not paid twice.
