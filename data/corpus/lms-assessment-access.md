# Assessments: access and deadlines

Assessments open in the LMS on their release date shown in the module calendar.

## Deadlines
All submissions close at 23:59 SAST on the due date. Late submissions are not marked unless an extension was approved.

## Extensions
You can request an assessment extension of up to 7 days with supporting documents, such as a medical certificate. Apply before the due date in the LMS under Assessments > Request extension.
