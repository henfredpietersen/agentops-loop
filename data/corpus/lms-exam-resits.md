# Exam re-sits

If you fail a module exam you may book one re-sit per module. Book the re-sit in the LMS under Exams > Book re-sit within 14 days of results being published.

The re-sit fee is listed on the fee schedule. Re-sit results replace the original mark, capped at 50%.
