# Refund Policy (effective 1 Feb 2025)

Kestrel Online Academy wants students to enrol with confidence. This policy replaces all earlier refund rules.

## Cooling-off period
Students may cancel within 30 days of enrolment for a full refund of tuition paid. The deposit is included in the full refund.

## Cancelling after 30 days
After the cooling-off period a pro-rata refund applies to modules you have not started, minus an admin fee of R450. Modules already opened in the LMS are not refundable.

## How to ask for a refund
Submit the refund form in the student portal under Billing > Refunds. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 10 business days.
