# Proctoring review - staff procedure

## Flagged attempts
Flagged exam attempts are reviewed by an invigilator within 3 business days.

## Reopening an attempt
Staff can reopen an exam attempt from the admin console under Attempts > Reopen when a student lost connection and the proctoring log confirms it.
